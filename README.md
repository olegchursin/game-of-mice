# 🐭 Chronicles of the Twin Mouse Houses — Adventure & Smart Quests

An iPhone & iPad adventure and scholarly quest game inspired directly by the two real mouse dollhouses in [`ideas/`](file:///Users/olegchursin/dev/game-of-mice/ideas):
1. **House 1: The Slanted-Roof Timber House** (`IMG_3710.jpg` – `IMG_3716.jpg`) — Natural birch plywood with a slanted roof, two vintage *Havana Cigars* wooden box beds, the carpenter's penciled `75,29` mark in the royal attic, handmade cardboard front-loading washing machine, *A. R. Dean & Sons* antique newspaper clipping, woven floral bassinet, and cellar apothecary jars.
2. **House 2: The White Gable Manor & Roadster Courtyard** (`IMG_3718.jpg` – `IMG_3725.jpg`) — White pitched-roof manor with a gourmet kitchen (pastel pink retro fridge, stacked jam jars, red *Parm* cheese box, golden kettle), Crimson Rose parlor with wheeled rover, Sprinkled Donut & Crystal Geode elephant bath, Giant Silver Keychain Clasp wardrobe & 9 fuzzy critters, Handwritten Wall Scroll & Unicorn study, Sailor Mouse's bunk & Silver Crescent-Moon cradle, and the **Vintage Burgundy Convertible Roadster** parked outside!

## ✨ Features

- **4 Playable Mouse Companions**:
  - ⚓️ **Pippin Sailor-Stripe** (*Math & Navigation* — from `IMG_3725.jpg`)
  - 👑 **Princess Rosalind of 75,29** (*Linguistics & Ciphers* — from `IMG_3714.jpg`)
  - 🧐 **Prof. Barnaby Havana** (*History & Archives* — from `IMG_3712.jpg`)
  - 🔐 **Clover Key-Clasp** (*Logic & Deduction* — from `IMG_3721.jpg`)
- **16 Authentic Rooms × 4 Disciplines = 64 Smart Quests per Tier (128 Total Quests!)**:
  - Every room contains tailored quests in **🔢 Math**, **🧩 Logic**, **🗣️ Linguistics**, and **🏛️ History**.
- **2 Selectable Difficulty Tiers**:
  - 🌱 **Junior Explorer (Ages 8–11)**
  - 🎓 **Master Scholar (Ages 12+ & Adult)**
- **Dual Visual Modes (Storybook Art + Real Dollhouse Photo Inspector)**:
  - Toggle seamlessly between custom vector Storybook Dollhouse Art and your real `IMG_3710`–`IMG_3725` photos with interactive magnifying-glass clue pins!

## 🚀 How to Play

### Option A: Instant Playable iPhone / iPad Web & PWA App (`web/`)
Open [`web/index.html`](file:///Users/olegchursin/dev/game-of-mice/web/index.html) directly in Safari, Chrome, or on an iPad/iPhone, or serve locally:
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory /Users/olegchursin/dev/game-of-mice/web
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8080` (on iPhone/iPad Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** for full-screen standalone mode).

### Option B: Native SwiftUI iPhone & iPad App (`MouseHouseQuest/`)
Open [`MouseHouseQuest/MouseHouseQuest.xcodeproj`](file:///Users/olegchursin/dev/game-of-mice/MouseHouseQuest/MouseHouseQuest.xcodeproj) in Xcode, select any iPhone or iPad Simulator (or your physical device), and press **Run (`⌘R`)**.
